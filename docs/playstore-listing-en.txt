FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------

Eagle Animation is a free, open-source stop motion animation studio. Shoot
your film frame by frame, preview it instantly, and export it as a video.
No ads, no account, no paid upgrade - every feature is included.

Built by Brick a Brack, the non-profit behind Brickfilms.com, for LEGO
brickfilms, clay animation, paper cut-outs and anything else you can move
one frame at a time.


ANIMATE WITH THE RIGHT TOOLS

- Onion skin: see the previous frames through the live view and nail every
  movement.
- Difference mode: instantly spot what moved between two frames.
- Grid and guides: classic grid, center lines and margins, with adjustable
  opacity, lines and columns.
- Live playback: play, loop, or short play the last frames to check your
  animation without leaving the camera.
- Friendly timeline: duplicate, deduplicate, hide, reorder or delete frames
  in a single tap.


SHOOT LIKE A PRO

- Full manual camera control: exposure, shutter speed, ISO, aperture, white
  balance, focus, brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpness and zoom.
- Frame averaging: capture several shots and merge them automatically to
  remove picture noise.
- Masking: capture two shots and paint away what you want to hide - no green
  screen needed.
- Aspect ratios: 4:3, 16:9, cinema, square, story, social media or your own
  custom ratio.
- Multi-scene projects: split your film into scenes and keep everything
  organised.


EXPORT YOUR FILM

- Export to video in H264, HEVC, ProRes, VP8 or VP9, up to your camera's
  full resolution.
- Choose your animation framerate, and a different output framerate if you
  need one.
- Automatically duplicate the first and last frames, or any duplicated
  frame.
- Export the raw frames as JPEG, PNG or WEBP to finish your film in any
  video editor.


ANIMATE EVERYWHERE

Eagle Animation also runs on Windows, macOS, Linux and directly in your web
browser, so you can start on your phone and carry on at your desk.

Available in 23 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian,
Portuguese, Polish, Dutch, Russian, Czech, Slovak, Slovenian, Croatian,
Romanian, Hungarian, Bulgarian, Greek, Danish, Swedish, Finnish, Latvian,
Lithuanian and Esperanto.
